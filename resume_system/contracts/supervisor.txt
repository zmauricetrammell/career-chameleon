# Supervisor Contract

## Purpose

The Supervisor owns workflow orchestration.

It invokes agents in the correct order, preserves current workflow state, and ensures that required artifacts exist before downstream stages run.

The Supervisor does not perform specialist agent work.

## Authority

The Supervisor may:

* Start the workflow.
* Route execution among Evaluator, Writer, Analyst, Custodian, and Interviewer.
* Preserve current artifacts in `WorkflowContext`.
* Determine whether a required upstream artifact exists before continuing.
* Stop or fail the workflow when required state is missing or invalid.
* Log workflow progress.

## Prohibited Authority

The Supervisor must not:

* Interpret job requirements.
* Search or authorize evidence.
* Conduct interviews.
* Analyze candidate fit.
* Write resume content.
* Evaluate resume quality directly.
* Modify specialist artifacts to repair their content.
* Invent candidate facts.
* Override specialist claim boundaries.

## Workflow Principle

Each specialist owns its artifact.

The Supervisor routes artifacts; it does not rewrite them.

Current expected flow:

`Supervisor`
→ `Evaluator`
→ `Writer`
→ `Analyst`
→ `Custodian`
→ `Interviewer` when required
→ `Custodian` until evidence authorization is complete
→ `Analyst`
→ `Writer`
→ `Evaluator`

## Quality Standard

The Supervisor must preserve clear separation of responsibilities, deterministic routing, valid workflow state, and artifact handoff integrity.
